Before you join

A few practical answers about coaching and the community.

Which level should I choose?

The curriculum has Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced 1 and Advanced 2 levels. Ask the coach to help choose a suitable starting point based on your current experience. See the course curriculum for each level's learning objectives.

How are lessons arranged?

The academy offers personalised one-to-one coaching. Beginner and Intermediate classes are 45 minutes; Advanced 1 and Advanced 2 classes are 50 minutes. Parents and the coach agree on class times. Contact the coach to confirm the available lesson format and schedule before booking.

What are the fees?

Current fees are not published on this website. Ask about fees and availability before making a booking. The curriculum lists packages of 24 classes, with a maximum validity of four months per package.

How do I request a demo class?

Complete the demo request form. It opens WhatsApp so you can send your request to the coaching team. The coach will confirm timing and any applicable demo terms; submitting the form does not automatically confirm a booking.

Do I need coaching to join the community?

No. The community welcomes all skill levels. You can join discussions, explore free puzzles and videos, and follow event announcements without enrolling in the academy. Explore the community.
