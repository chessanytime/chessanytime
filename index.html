About the academy

Hello everyone, I’m Shubham, a chess coach and competitive player. Chess AnyTime shares lessons, game analysis, endgame techniques and practical improvement strategies for players rated below 2100.

Lessons

One-to-one coaching built around your games and your rating.

Game analysis

Review your own games to find the mistakes that cost you points.

Endgames and strategy

Endgame techniques and practical improvement strategies you can use in your next game.

Community

A place for Chess AnyTime students and followers to play, learn and share games.

Practice games

Regular practice games and online arenas with other students.

Study groups

Small groups working through openings, tactics and endgames together.

Events and tournaments

Friendly tournaments and meetups for every level up to 2100.

Student spotlight

Wins, rating milestones and best games from our students.
