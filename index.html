What you’ll learn at each level

♞

Beginner

Objectives

Learn to play from setup to checkmate.

  • Set up the board and record moves.
  • Learn how each piece moves and attacks.
  • Escape threats by moving, blocking or capturing.
  • Spot checkmate and stalemate.
  • Castle, promote and capture en passant.
  • Find loose pieces and good exchanges.
  • Develop pieces and spot pins and forks.
  • Practise rook and queen mates. Review your games.
♜

Intermediate

Objectives

Spot tactics and judge basic endgames.

  • Win material with pins, skewers and x-rays.
  • Spot double attacks, discovered checks and windmills.
  • Distract or overload a defender.
  • Find mating threats and mates in two.
  • Use the back rank and seventh rank.
  • Find key squares, breakthroughs and zugzwang.
  • Know when to simplify or underpromote.
  • Save draws by stalemate or perpetual check.
♛

Advanced 1

Objectives

Plan attacks and convert positional advantages.

  • Study queen against a seventh-rank pawn.
  • Use triangulation and corresponding squares.
  • Attack with a lead in development.
  • Calculate sacrifices and opposite-side pawn storms.
  • Find outposts and active defences.
  • Assess passed pawns against bishops and rooks.
  • Read pawn structures in piece endings.
  • Explore Ruy López, French, Caro-Kann and Sicilian plans.
♚

Advanced 2

Objectives

Build an opening repertoire and calculate tournament lines.

  • Prepare Ruy López, Najdorf and Italian lines.
  • Compare Indian defences, Grünfeld and Benoni.
  • Study Catalan, English and Slav structures.
  • Test your openings in practice games.
  • Calculate lines that expose the king.
  • Improve your pieces and anticipate threats.
  • Use pawn majorities and challenge the centre.
  • Activate the king and exploit two weaknesses.
